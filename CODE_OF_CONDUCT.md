# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant to foster an open, welcoming, and respectful community.

## Table of contents

- [Our Pledge](#our-pledge)
- [Standards](#standards)
- [Reporting](#reporting)

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive place for everyone. By participating, you agree to follow these guidelines.

## Standards

- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Treat others with kindness; disagree without demeaning anyone.
- No harassment, hate speech, or abusive behavior.
- Respect privacy — do not share private or sensitive information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open an issue titled "Code of Conduct report" or contact the maintainers so we can address it promptly. Reports will be handled with discretion.

When reporting, please include as much of the following as you can:

- A short description of the incident
- Links, screenshots, or logs if available
- The usernames involved (if applicable)

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and may take action including warnings, temporary or permanent bans, or removal of content to keep the community safe.

## Contact

If you need to contact the maintainers privately, please use the repository's issue mechanism and mark the issue "Code of Conduct report", or add a maintainer contact to the repository README for private reports.
